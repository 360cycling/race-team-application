360 JRT · Junior Race Team

The blue kit

Applying takes two minutes, at the bottom of this page ↓

Who it's for

Racing age 17 or 18 in 2027. British Cycling racing age is your age on 31 December 2027, so riders born 2009 or 2010.

The racing

Racing at regional, national and selected junior UCI races, with the Quanta Capital Junior Tour of Ireland as a major focus. The full programme is shared when you apply.

The aim

Learn to race properly, get seen, and arrive at U23 level already knowing how a hard race works.

The Junior Race Team develops riders through their junior years: briefed starts, a car behind the race, and a team that rides together. Bring the engine, we'll teach the craft.

360 U23 · Under-23 Team

The red kit

Applying takes two minutes, at the bottom of this page ↓

Who it's for

Riders from racing age 18 up. Primarily focused on under-23 development, with offers considered for over-23s.

The racing

Hard racing at national level in the UK with a European element. The full programme is shared when you apply.

The aim

Race where it's hardest, measure yourself honestly, and put results next to your name.

The red kit races where it's hardest, in the UK and on the continent. Podiums came home in this kit in 2026, and the bar is set there.

What you get

Built like a pro team,
run for riders

Every part of the programme exists to take a job off your shoulders so the only thing left to do is race.

Race-day operation

Team car in the convoy, spares on the roof, gazebo up, bottles done. You warm up; the machine runs around you.

Race intelligence

Every A-race gets a proper brief: course notes, key sectors, wind, a plan A and a plan B.

Coaching that watches

Structured feedback, testing days and numbers reviewed by people who know what they mean.

A European education

Selected French and European races, with race blocks where appropriate. Experience you cannot get at home.

The camp

A warm-weather team camp to build the winter engine and turn a roster into a team before the flag drops.

People in your corner

From sign-on to the last barrier, someone has your back. Ask the riders. Better: come and find out.

The record so far

In 2026 the team raced junior and under-23 calendars at national level in the UK, and the under-23 squad completed a European racing block. Podium results came home from France. Full results are available to riders and parents on request.

Who we're looking for

Selection is about more than watts

The team looks for riders who want to develop, and who make the group stronger.

Junior riders

  • Racing experience appropriate to the junior calendar
  • Commitment to structured training
  • Willingness to learn race craft
  • Reliability and good communication
  • The support of a parent or guardian

U23 riders

  • Strong racing ambition and appropriate experience
  • Willingness to work for teammates
  • Coachability and a good attitude
  • Commitment to a full season
  • Clear communication

Not sure whether your experience fits? Apply anyway and set out where you are. Every application is read.
