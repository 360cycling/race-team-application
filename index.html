The Junior Race Team

The junior team, and how to join it for 2027. Use the toggle to view the Under-23 team.

360 JRT · Junior Race Team

Applying takes two minutes, at the bottom of this page ↓

Who it's for

Racing age 17–18 in 2027 (born 2009 or 2010).

The racing

Regional, national and selected junior UCI races. The Quanta Capital Junior Tour of Ireland is a major focus.

The aim

Develop the skills, experience and race craft needed to make the step up to U23 and senior racing.

The Junior Race Team develops riders through their junior years: briefed starts, a car behind the race, and a team that rides together.

360 U23 · Under-23 Team

Applying takes two minutes, at the bottom of this page ↓

Who it's for

Racing age 18+. Under-23 focused, with offers considered for over-23s.

The racing

National-level UK racing with a European element.

The aim

Race at the front and build towards higher-level racing.

The under-23 team races in the UK and on the continent. Podiums came home from France in 2026.

What you get

Race support

Race-day logistics and support are organised by the team, so riders can focus on racing.

Race-day operation

Team car in the convoy, spares, gazebo, bottles and warm-up organised.

Race briefing

Key races get a full brief: course, sectors, wind and a plan.

Coaching & review

Structured coaching, testing and performance review.

European racing

Selected French and European races, and race blocks where appropriate.

Team camp

A warm-weather training camp to build the winter engine as a team.

Staff & teammates

Staff and teammates with you from sign-on to the finish.

The junior record so far

Through 2026 the junior riders raced local and regional races alongside the wider 360 squad, and trained together over the winter on the team's weekly group rides. 360 ran indoor sessions at Tameside right through the winter. For 2027 the plan is to add some of the flatter junior UCI races to the programme.

The U23 record so far

In 2026 the under-23 squad raced national-level calendars in the UK and completed a European racing block. Podium results came home from France. Full results are available to riders and parents on request.

Inside a 360 race brief

The level of detail riders get for a key race

Pages from a team rider brief: course boards, sector-by-sector analysis and the points that decide the race.

Who we're looking for

What we look for in a rider

Selection is not only about power numbers. The team looks for riders who want to develop and who make the group stronger.

Junior riders

  • Commitment to training and improving
  • Willingness to learn race craft and tactics
  • Ability to listen, take feedback and develop
  • Positive attitude within a team environment
  • Reliable, organised and good at communicating

U23 riders

  • Commitment to a demanding training and race programme
  • Strong motivation to progress in the sport
  • Ability to contribute to team objectives
  • Coachable with a professional attitude
  • Reliable, organised and good at communicating
