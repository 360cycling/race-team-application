360 JRT · Junior Race Team

The blue kit

Applications are at the bottom of this page

Who it's for

Racing age 17 or 18 in 2027. British Cycling racing age is your age on 31 December 2027, so riders born 2009 or 2010.

The racing

Junior racing at national level, home and abroad, raced as a team with a plan. The full programme is shared when you apply.

The aim

Learn to race properly, get seen, and arrive at U23 level already knowing how a hard race works.

The Junior Race Team develops riders through their junior years: briefed starts, a car behind the race, and a team that rides together. The aim is to produce complete bike racers, prepared for the next level.

360 U23 · Under-23 Team

The red kit

Applications are at the bottom of this page

Who it's for

Riders from racing age 18 up. Primarily focused on under-23 development, with offers considered for over-23s.

The racing

Hard racing at national level in the UK with a European element. The full programme is shared when you apply.

The aim

Race where it's hardest, measure yourself honestly, and put results next to your name.

The Under-23 team races at national level in the UK with a European element. Podiums came home in this kit in 2026. Selection and support are built around long-term development as well as results.

The programme

What the team provides

The programme is built so that riders can concentrate on racing. What follows applies across both squads, adjusted to age and level.

Race programme

  • National A races
  • National B races
  • U23 National Road Series
  • Selected European races
  • Stage races
  • Appropriate UCI opportunities where applicable

Race support

  • Directeur Sportif support
  • Mechanics
  • Feed support
  • Team vehicles
  • Race-day organisation
  • Post-race support at selected events

European racing

  • Selected French and European races
  • Race blocks where appropriate
  • Experience gained outside the UK

The record so far

In 2026 the team raced junior and under-23 calendars at national level in the UK, and the under-23 squad completed a European racing block including French elite nationals, Belgian kermesse racing and Paris–Troyes. Podium results came home from France. Full results are available to riders and parents on request.

Development

How riders progress

Development at 360 is structured and long term. The measure of a season is the rider it produces, not only the results column.

Coaching & development

  • Structured coaching
  • Performance review
  • Training planning
  • Rider development
  • Race preparation

Team environment

  • Team tactics
  • Rider communication
  • Professional expectations
  • Development of race craft
  • Working as part of a team

Progression

  • Junior to U23 development
  • Building riders towards higher-level racing
  • Long-term development, not only individual results

Who we're looking for

Selection is about more than watts

Selection is not based only on power numbers or individual results. The team looks for riders who want to develop, and who make the group stronger.

Junior riders

Racing age 17 or 18 in 2027.

  • Racing experience appropriate to the junior calendar
  • Commitment to structured training
  • Willingness to learn race craft
  • Reliability and good communication
  • The support of a parent or guardian

U23 riders

Racing age 18 and up, focused on under-23 development.

  • Strong racing ambition and appropriate experience
  • Willingness to work for teammates
  • Coachability and a good attitude
  • Commitment to a full season
  • Clear communication

If you are not sure whether your experience fits, apply anyway and set out where you are. Every application is read.
